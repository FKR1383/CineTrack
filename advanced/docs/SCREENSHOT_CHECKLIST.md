# Submission screenshot checklist

Capture at least:

1. Login/register and guest entry.
2. Home with real TMDB posters and all five sections.
3. Search for a title not present in the original demo catalog (for example Interstellar).
4. Actor/director/genre/year search filters.
5. Movie detail with provider rating, CineTrack rating, cast/director and poster.
6. Series detail with seasons/episodes.
7. Episode watch marking + progress percentage/color.
8. Watch status and favorites.
9. Rating 1–5 and distribution.
10. Comment, spoiler hidden/reveal, edit/delete/report.
11. Personal lists.
12. Profile statistics/activity.
13. Admin users/reports/comments/cache/statistics.
14. Swagger `/docs`.
15. `/api/v1/media/provider-status` showing TMDB configured/live search.
